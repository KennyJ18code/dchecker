Range rules

Rules are off until you tick them on. Each rule has a green band (normal) and a yellow band (caution); outside the yellow band is red. Leave a bound blank for no limit. Comp running skips the rule while the compressor is off; Settle waits that many minutes after the compressor starts. Rules save in this browser automatically.

    Fault chips (amber) score how many of a classic fault's conditions hold. Signatures use fixed engineering thresholds (EV within 3% of its log maximum for 3 min, SC 3°F off target after 8 min settle, discharge SH under 25°F at ≥50% speed, 5°F/min discharge climb, PT-table disagreement over 4°F, sensor disagreement over 15 psi, coil 12°F from saturation, current/rps 15% over the log's steady median, runs under 5 min or off-times under 3 min). They are hints, not verdicts.
